Privacy Policy — PhotoTools Apps

Last updated: March 17, 2026

This privacy policy applies to all apps published by ByteForge:

  • Photo Compressor to 1MB
  • HEIC to JPG Converter
  • Metadata Cleaner
  • Bulk Image Resizer
  • Image to PDF
  • PDF Compressor

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.

All processing happens entirely on your device. Your photos and files are never uploaded to any server.

Permissions

Our apps use the Photo Picker API, which means:

  • We never access your full photo library
  • We only see the specific photos you select
  • No storage permissions are requested on Android 10+

Third-Party Services

Google Play Billing

Used to process one-time Pro purchases. Google handles payment data — we never see your payment information.

Google AdMob (Free version only)

Displays non-personalized banner ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad serving. You can opt out via your device's ad settings. Pro users see no ads.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect anonymous crash reports and diagnostics. This helps us identify and fix bugs. Data collected includes device model, OS version, and crash stack traces. No personal data is included. You can opt out via your device's Firebase settings.

Google Play In-App Review

We may ask you to rate the app after your second successful operation. This uses Google's standard review flow.

Data Stored on Device

  • Pro purchase status (locally)
  • Daily usage count (resets daily)
  • Successful operation count for review prompts

No data is transmitted to any server.
